What I'm tracking

The shifts shaping enterprise technology delivery

These are the topics I follow closely because they're changing how large programs get scoped, governed and shipped.

AGENTS

Agentic AI is entering the enterprise

MCP and tool calling are turning assistants into systems that take actions. Integration design and permissions are now program-level decisions.

RAG

Retrieval in regulated industries

Grounding, data lineage and evaluation used to be engineering details. In banking they're governance requirements that have to be planned from day one.

COST

Usage forecasting, model choice and consumption tracking are becoming budget line items, and onboarding decisions depend on them.
